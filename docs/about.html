About

A student library, hosted by GoTVET.

GoTVET is a free way for South African TVET students to find and download past papers. Use it from any device — no login and no payment.

What is here now

The library lists NC(V) Levels 2, 3 and 4 from the DHET subject matrix: three fundamental subjects for every student, plus the core and optional vocational subjects for each programme. Past papers cover February and November from 2011 to 2026, with a question paper and memorandum for each sitting.

How it is organised

Filter by programme, subject, level, year or session on this website, or use the same filters in the Windows app after choosing Refresh library.
